

Change Log:
06.10.2026
Design & Navigation
- Unified the design across the dashboard, dialogs, sidebar, and spreadsheet tabs.
- Improved column widths, alignment, headers, and theme-based colours.
- Reorganized the Herd Management menu.
- Added contextual help, tooltips, and a central Help & Guide.
Themes & Branding
- Added four built-in themes.
- Added a Theme Editor with custom colours, previews, contrast hints, and saved themes.
- Added customizable stud names, signatures, logos, and dashboard subtitles.
Dashboard
- Updated the layout, charts, icons, and typography.
- Improved herd statistics, occupancy information, and data handling.
- Standardized interface text in English.
Breeding & Genetics
- Improved Foal Calculator loading speed and horse selection.
- Improved coat-colour calculations and result grouping.
- Made additional colour outcomes expandable.
- Added shared-ancestor information and explanations.
- Clearly separated local pedigree-based inbreeding estimates from official HR COI values.
- Added support for manually recording HR COI values for individual pairings.
- Updated Outside Studs to support the Breed column and flag missing breeds.
Spreadsheet Improvements
- Applied consistent styling across horse, stats, genetics, pedigree, breeding, market, and settings tabs.
- Added frozen headers and identifying columns where useful.
- Improved result matrices with wrapped names and dynamic styling for new horse columns.
- Added blank-ID checks to selected formulas to reduce errors in empty rows.
- Prevented invalid foaling-date calculations when the covering date is missing.
- Corrected the Competition Team sync’s starting row.
Archive
- Added clearer formatting and collapsible groups by source tab.
- Made archive styling adapt to additional columns.
- Fixed failures when removing the last unfrozen row or column.
- Improved recovery after interrupted archiving and prevented duplicate archive entries on retry.
- Added Archive All Horses, with explicit confirmation, using the existing archive workflow for every horse in Herd.
Fixes
- Corrected several number-font and header-alignment issues.
- Fixed styling interruptions caused by number-format changes in typed columns.
- Improved styling error messages so they remain readable until dismissed.
